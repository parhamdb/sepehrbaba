## Finding

I found no clearly better cross-gap pair. The only plausible candidate is the broad tile-floor color/seam constellation in `frame_006491.jpg` at 412.170167 s and `frame_006704.jpg` at 423.695311 s. Both show beige tiles, grout, and teal/green coloring; a nearby brown/gray mark might be useful only if its shape and position relative to a specific seam can be tracked through the floor-facing gap frames. I could not verify that distinctive correspondence in the originals. The color may be illumination, reflection, or shadow, and the views are blurred and differently oriented. I therefore rank it **low confidence / unresolved**, not as an improved match.

The gap includes a short floor-facing sweep around 414.012–414.513 s (`frame_006520.jpg`–`frame_006530.jpg`), but those views do not visibly establish the particular before-to-after scuff correspondence. They are a possible intermediate A→C→B bridge only, not direct evidence of one.

The many similar black bags, white coverings, and purple cloth configurations were excluded as repetitive context. I did not use faces, body features, or identities. No coordinates are supplied as accepted geometry; the JSON contains broad search boxes for the weak floor candidate only.
