Why the promising 08:50 result is not yet a connection

Both sides have cameras in the same COLMAP component. A single alignment fitted before the gap disagrees with cameras afterward. Fitting the sides separately also changes the DA3 scale factor by about 42%, and VGGT's by about 65%. This suggests drift or inconsistent reconstruction; COLMAP itself is not ground truth. The source briefly becomes almost fully occluded, then badly overexposed. These results do not establish a continuous path.

Unlike the earlier ranking, this test evaluates each method against all its available reference cameras and fits only alternating pre-gap cameras. Its numbers must not be treated as a new like-for-like method ranking.

The floor trial uses manually reviewed bare-floor regions before the gap. It excludes people, bags and the wheeled trolley. Triangulated floor landmarks and withheld image observations test whether the gap can be recovered without trusting a learned trajectory. A failed positive control leaves localization blocked; it is not evidence that every possible floor matcher would fail.
